Cardiology

Cardiology is the branch of medicine that deals with diseases and disorders of the heart and blood vessels.

Treatments: ECGs, echocardiograms, angioplasty, heart failure management, medication for hypertension and arrhythmias.

Neurology

We deal with a medical field that specializes in the diagnosis, treatment, and management of disorders and diseases affecting the brain, spinal cord, and nervous system.

We will give you the best treatment

Hematology

Hematology is a medical field that deals with study, diagnosis, treatment and prevention of disorders related to the blood, bone marrow, lymphatic system and blood-forming organs.

Treatments: Chemotherapy for blood cancers, blood transfusions, management of anemia, sickle cell diseases, bleeding diseases and clotting disorders.

Pediatrics

Pediatricians monitor growth and development, diagnose and treat childhood illnesses, administer vaccinations, and provide guidance on nutrition, safety, and emotional well-being.

Treatments: Vaccinations, growth monitoring, treatment of infections, congenital disorder management.

Ophthalmology (Eye Care)

Eye health and vision

Ophthalmologists diagnose and treat eye diseases such as cataracts, glaucoma, macular degeneration, and refractive errors (like myopia and astigmatism).
